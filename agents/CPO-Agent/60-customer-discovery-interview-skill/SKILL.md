# 60 Customer Discovery Interview Skill

## What this skill does
Runs a structured Customer Discovery & Problem Validation interview that helps a founder validate a real customer problem before building a product or feature — strictly separating Problem Discovery (Questions 1–13) from Solution Validation (Question 14+), scoring the interview, and aggregating results across interviews into a validation dashboard.

## Source Modules
- 178_Customer_Discovery_Interview_OS

## Output
- Interview Record (Customer/Company, Interviewee Role, Industry, Date, Interviewer)
- Context, Pain (severity 1–5), Business Impact, Current Solution, Buying Reality (User/Champion/Decision Maker/Budget Owner)
- Commitment & Next Step (No Interest, Follow-up Meeting, Product Demo, Technical Evaluation, Pilot, Commercial Discussion)
- Founder Score (Pain, Frequency, Business Impact, Current Spend, Buying Intent — /25 total) and Recommendation (KILL / INVESTIGATE / VALIDATE-BUILD)
- Most Important Customer Sentence (verbatim quote)
- Validation Dashboard aggregating repeated problems, pain, frequency, business impact, alternatives, spend, decision makers, budget owners, buying signals, and common quotes across interviews

## Critical Rule
The founder must not pitch, describe, demonstrate, or defend the product before Question 14. This skill enforces that separation structurally, not just as a suggestion.
